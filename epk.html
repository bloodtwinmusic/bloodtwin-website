blood.twin
(Pronounced "Blood Twin" the dot is silent)

Goth / Darkwave / Industrial / Post-Punk / Deathrock / EBM / Coldwave

Emerging from the concrete brutalism of London's underground, blood.twin is D.I.Y., there is no band. There is only a singular, anonymous entity weaving an aural tapestry that is both unconventionally beautiful and chillingly intense. Never following trends or fads he absorbs influences permanently, carrying their entire lived history forward as a constant, monolithic force.
